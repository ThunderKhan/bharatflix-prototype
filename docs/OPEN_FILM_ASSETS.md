## About player thumbnail imagery

For entries with a linked official Blender YouTube ID, the wide background is the corresponding `i.ytimg.com` thumbnail. The embedded video uses `youtube-nocookie.com` and leaves the YouTube interface intact; the platform does not proxy or download videos. If embedding is disabled upstream, the fallback is the licensed MDN sample clip.

## Limitations

- The remote Wikimedia and YouTube thumbnail URLs require network access and can become unavailable or change; replace them with rights-cleared, appropriately cached assets before presenting offline.
- IMDb/TMDB audience ratings are **not** invented; Open Cinema items intentionally carry no fake numeric rating.
- The remaining concept films and all series are fictional, retaining their original artwork and episode data.
- Open-film video entries are not a license to publish unrelated Hollywood/Bollywood titles.
- Downloaded films, YouTube stream extraction, DRM bypasses, and unlicensed sources are not implemented.

**Trademark note:** Original poster files sometimes contain logos not independently released under Creative Commons; do not crop to focus on any logo or treat Blender/other marks as BharatFlix endorsements.
